Validation controls

Important limitations

This application receives public tick data. It cannot access Deriv's internal random generator or predict its state. A chi-square result cannot establish that a digit is predictable. High hit rates do not prove profitability: contract payouts, transaction costs and the true probability must be considered. No trades are executed by this page.
